I am an economist trained at the Madras School of Economics, starting the two-year MSc Economics at the London School of Economics in September 2026. My interests sit in macroeconomics — how aggregate fluctuations propagate, and how much of the cycle we can actually identify from the data we have.

Most of my work so far has been on the empirical side of that question: time-series econometrics, unit roots and cointegration, ARMA and VAR models, spectral methods, and the estimation problems that come with them. More recently I have been moving toward machine-learning methods — working through Murphy's Probabilistic Machine Learning and closing the gap between writing Python and writing models — because a lot of the interesting macro-data questions are now high-dimensional ones.

I also read mathematics for its own sake. I taught myself complex analysis from Churchill and Brown, and spent a month in 2026 reading around the Riemann Hypothesis and writing up what I understood, from the Euler product through to the functional equation.
