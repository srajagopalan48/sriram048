Applied Econometrics · 2025

Empirical validity of the Phillips curve in India

Tests the inflation–unemployment relationship in India using national annual time-series and an unbalanced state-level panel (rural and urban, 2017–23). With Breusch–Pagan LM and F-tests both indicating no significant panel effects, the paper estimates pooled OLS with year dummies and state-clustered standard errors, alongside an expectations-augmented specification with lagged inflation. Finds no evidence of the Phillips curve — the unemployment coefficient stays positive and insignificant, with inflation instead driven by supply-side (year) shocks and inflation persistence.

Stochastic Processes · 2025

Hamilton's regime-switching models

A rigorous exposition of Markov-switching time-series models, working through the derivations from Neftçi (1984, Journal of Political Economy) and Goldfeld–Quandt (1973, Journal of Econometrics) to Hamilton (1989) — the finite-state Markov structure, the AR(1) representation of the latent regime, and the nonlinear filter and smoother. Implemented in Python to recover smoothed regime probabilities for US Real GNP.

Understanding the Riemann Hypothesis

A step-by-step development, from the Euler product through analytic continuation, the Gamma function, the theta identity, and the completed zeta function and its functional equation, ending with the zero-free line Re(s) = 1 and an expository section linking the Riemann Hypothesis to the Prime Number Theorem. Written after independent study of complex analysis from Brown and Churchill, with Stein and Shakarchi as the main reference for the final two sections.

2026 · Political philosophy

Rousseau and modern India: eight decades of a large democracy

An essay set against Rousseau's argument in The Social Contract that democracy requires a small, homogeneous, virtuous people. India is none of these, and the piece argues that its Constitution and independent institutions have done work Rousseau did not foresee — while its civil service has behaved much as he warned an unaccountable administrative class would.
